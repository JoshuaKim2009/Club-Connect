What's happening?

The embedding model converts each piece of text into a numerical representation called an embedding.

Texts with similar meanings tend to produce vectors that point in similar directions.

We then calculate the cosine similarity between the two vectors.

For example: 

"robotics"
       ↓
[0.12, -0.83, 0.41, 0.09, ...]

"building autonomous machines"
       ↓
[0.15, -0.79, 0.38, 0.12, ...]

              ↓

       Cosine Similarity

              ↓

            HIGH

Notice that the two sentences don't need to contain the same words. That's the major difference between this and simple keyword matching or TF-IDF.
